Keys: ←→ = steer · Z / Space = accelerate (or hold a finger on the screen) · X or ↓ = brake, and with ←→ a skid turn · ↑ = boost (one per lap) · Shift = engine sound on/off · Enter = race again after the flag · Enter + Shift together steps through the modes: race, practice (Shift / Enter bend the road), two-player link (needs two consoles and a cable, so not this page) · F = fullscreen · gamepads supported

On the grid, ←→ set how quick the rivals are (the bar between LO and TOP); Z, Space or Enter starts the countdown. The top two settings are 5 laps instead of 3, and a win moves you up one. The rail and rivals cost health; the white strip after the start line recharges it. The dark ramp in the left lane is a tunnel short cut. Flashing strips are dash plates; the black strip is a jump plate. Corners throw you wide with the square of your speed, so brake for them; hitting the rail costs most of your speed. Rough dark patches are dirt, blocks are barriers, flashing white patches are boost pads. A mistake lets a rival pounce. The little car along the top of the sky shows how far round the lap you are. The lap has its own places to learn: the Fork (tunnel left or boost right), the Dirt Hairpin (no inside line), the Causeway Slalom over water, and the Underpass (hug a wall).
